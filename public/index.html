Questions, answered.

What does Grammerly-ish do?

It takes text you've written and rewrites it in a clearer, more natural voice. It works on one passage at a time, and you copy the result out when you're happy with it.

How do I get an account?

Grammerly-ish is invite-only for now. Ask the person who shared the link with you to set up a login.

Is my text saved?

Grammerly-ish doesn't keep a history of what you paste or what comes back. To do the rewrite, your text is sent to a third-party rewriting service, so don't paste anything private or confidential.

Will the result pass an AI checker?

No tool can promise that. AI checkers disagree with each other and get things wrong, including on writing that was never touched by AI. That's why the Recheck button is there: see what a checker says, and edit the text yourself if something doesn't sound like you.

Can I use it for coursework?

Rules on AI writing tools differ between schools and even between modules. Check your institution's policy before you submit anything. You're responsible for what you hand in.

Why is there a text limit?

Each rewrite handles a limited amount of text. The app shows your limit as you type. For a longer essay, work through it one section at a time.
